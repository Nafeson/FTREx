Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Factory Toughened Rooflights collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quotation or place an order with us.

Section 01

Personal Information We May Collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • Your name and contact details, including your email address and telephone number.
  • Your billing address, delivery address and delivery postcode.
  • Information relating to an enquiry, quotation, rooflight specification or order.
  • Information you provide when contacting us by email, telephone, online messaging or other communication methods.
  • Transaction information, such as order value, payment status, transaction references and limited billing information.
  • Information required to arrange manufacturing, collection, delivery, installation or other services you request.
  • Technical information that may be generated when you use our website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, access times and security or server-log information.

We do not intentionally ask customers to provide special category personal information unless there is a genuine reason for it and we are permitted to process that information by law.

Section 02

How We Collect Your Information

Most personal information is provided directly by you when you contact us, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, configure a rooflight, place an order or communicate with our team.

We may also receive limited personal information from companies involved in providing services connected with your order, such as payment service providers, delivery providers, installers or other service providers.

Some technical information may be collected automatically when you access our website or interact with its systems.

Section 03

How We Use Your Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotations.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Manufacture rooflights to the specification you have requested.
  • Arrange delivery, collection, fitting or other requested services.
  • Contact you about an enquiry, quotation, order, delivery or service.
  • Process and confirm payments and refunds.
  • Maintain business, accounting and transaction records.
  • Handle customer service enquiries, complaints and disputes.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve our website and business systems.
  • Meet our legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations.
Section 04

Our Lawful Bases For Using Personal Information

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful reason for using personal information. Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on one or more of the following bases.

Contract. We may use your information where it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract with you. This includes providing quotations, processing orders, manufacturing products and arranging delivery or other requested services.

Legal obligation. We may process information where necessary to comply with obligations imposed on us by law, including certain tax, accounting and record-keeping requirements.

Legitimate interests. We may process information where necessary for legitimate business purposes, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests. This may include customer service, business administration, fraud prevention, website security, resolving disputes and protecting our legal rights.

Consent. Where the law requires us to obtain your consent for a particular use of your information, we will rely on that consent. You can withdraw consent at any time where consent is the lawful basis being used.

Section 05

Payments

Where you make an online payment, payment information may be processed by the payment service provider used by us at that time. The provider may collect information needed to authorise, process and manage the transaction.

Depending on the payment method used, we may receive information such as the payment status, transaction reference, amount paid and limited billing details.

Payment service providers process information under their own security systems and may also operate under their own privacy terms.

This Privacy Policy is deliberately not tied to a particular payment company so that the payment service used by the website can be changed without altering the underlying way we explain our handling of customer information.

Section 06

Who We May Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information.

Where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, we may share relevant personal information with organisations such as:

  • Payment service providers and financial institutions involved in processing a transaction.
  • Delivery companies, couriers and transport providers.
  • Installers, contractors or service providers involved in fulfilling a service requested by you.
  • Website hosting, IT, communications and other technology service providers.
  • Accountants, professional advisers, insurers and other business service providers.
  • Government bodies, regulators, law enforcement agencies or other authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We aim to provide third parties only with information that is reasonably necessary for the service they are providing or the purpose for which the information is being shared.

Section 07

International Transfers

Some third-party technology, communications or payment service providers may process or store information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure that an appropriate lawful mechanism or safeguard is used where required by UK data protection law.

This may include using providers in countries recognised as providing an adequate level of protection or using appropriate contractual safeguards.

Section 08

How Long We Keep Personal Information

We do not intend to keep personal information for longer than reasonably necessary.

The period for which information is retained depends on why it was collected, whether an order was placed, whether an enquiry or dispute remains active and whether we are required to retain records for legal, tax, accounting or regulatory purposes.

Order, invoice and transaction information may therefore be retained for longer than general enquiry information where this is necessary to meet legal or business record-keeping requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it as appropriate.

Section 09

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, secure systems, restricted access to information and the use of reputable third-party service providers.

No internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to use safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information being handled.

Section 10

Marketing Communications

If we send promotional or marketing communications, we will only do so where we have an appropriate lawful basis and where the communication is permitted by applicable law.

You can ask us to stop sending marketing communications at any time by using an unsubscribe option provided with the message where available, or by contacting us directly.

Stopping marketing communications does not prevent us from contacting you where necessary in connection with an existing enquiry, quotation, order, delivery, payment or other service.

Section 11

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as operating website features, maintaining preferences, security, performance and understanding how the website is used.

More detailed information about the cookies and similar technologies used by the website will be provided separately in our Cookies Policy.

Section 12

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis being used, UK data protection law may give you rights including:

  • The right to ask whether we hold personal information about you and to request access to it.
  • The right to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • The right to ask us to erase personal information in certain circumstances.
  • The right to ask us to restrict the way personal information is used in certain circumstances.
  • The right to object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • The right to data portability in circumstances where that right applies.
  • The right to withdraw consent where we rely on consent to process your information.

These rights are not absolute and may not apply in every situation. We may also need to confirm your identity before dealing with a request where this is reasonably necessary to protect personal information.

To make a data protection request, contact us at sales@factorytoughenedrooflights.co.uk .

Your Right To Object

Where we rely on legitimate interests to process your personal information, you may have the right to object to that processing. If you wish to object, contact us using the details provided in this Privacy Policy and explain the processing you are concerned about.

Section 13

Automated Decision-Making

Our website may automatically calculate matters such as product prices, dimensions, delivery charges or estimated delivery dates from information entered into the website.

These ordinary website calculations are not intended to constitute automated profiling or solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about an individual.

Section 14

Our website may contain links to websites, platforms or services operated by other organisations.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. If you follow a link to another website or service, you should review the privacy information provided by that organisation.
