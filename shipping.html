Shipping & Delivery

This Shipping & Delivery page explains our estimated production lead times, delivery scheduling, delivery areas and what to expect when receiving a Factory Toughened Rooflights order.

Shipping & Delivery

Estimated Lead Times

Production and delivery lead times shown on our website, provided with a quotation or communicated by our team are estimates unless we expressly confirm otherwise in writing.

Lead times are based on our typical manufacturing workload, product specification and delivery availability at the time of ordering. They may vary according to demand, glass availability, manufacturing requirements, order size, product options, transport availability or other circumstances affecting production or delivery.

Where we advertise production from 4 working days, this represents the earliest estimated production period for qualifying orders. It does not mean that every order will be manufactured, dispatched or delivered within four working days.

Any estimated lead time begins only once we have received the information and payment required to process the order and, where relevant, any specification or order details requiring clarification have been resolved.

Estimated Delivery Dates

Any delivery date, date range or estimated delivery information shown on our website, provided with a quotation, displayed during ordering or communicated by our team should be treated as an estimate unless we expressly agree a fixed delivery date in writing.

We make reasonable efforts to deliver within the estimated period, but manufacturing and transport schedules can occasionally change. Traffic conditions, road closures, vehicle availability, weather, route planning, manufacturing delays, supplier delays and other operational factors may affect the final delivery date.

Where an estimated delivery window changes, we will aim to provide updated information when reasonably practical.

An estimated delivery date or delivery window should not be treated as confirmation that the rooflight will definitely be available on site on that date.

Longer-Distance Deliveries

Delivery schedules are influenced by the distance between the delivery address and the routes available to our delivery network.

Deliveries to more distant locations, particularly addresses approximately 150 miles or more from our dispatch area, may require additional route planning, vehicle scheduling or consolidation with other deliveries.

For this reason, longer-distance orders may occasionally have a wider or later estimated delivery window than orders located closer to our dispatch area.

The 150-mile reference is a general delivery-planning indicator rather than a guaranteed boundary, fixed delivery time or separate service level. Delivery times can vary on either side of this distance.

Delivery Area & Charges

Delivery availability, delivery charges and maximum delivery distance may vary according to the size, weight and specification of the rooflight and the delivery postcode.

Where our website calculates a delivery charge or delivery availability from a postcode, that calculation is based on the information available to the website at that time and may be subject to verification where an order has unusual access, location, size, weight or transport requirements.

Some larger, extra-large or unusually heavy rooflights may be subject to a reduced delivery area, specialist transport arrangements or a separate delivery quotation.

Where a delivery address falls outside the delivery area available for a particular product, please contact us and we can advise whether an alternative delivery or collection arrangement may be possible.

Before Booking Installation

Because production and delivery dates are estimates, we strongly recommend waiting until your complete order has arrived on site and has been checked before scheduling any third-party installation services.

Important Installation Advice

Please wait until all items have been delivered to site and checked before booking builders, roofers, installers, lifting equipment, cranes, access equipment or any other third-party services. Except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, Factory Toughened Rooflights and Right Glaze Ltd cannot accept responsibility for third-party labour charges, cancellation charges, equipment-hire costs, loss of earnings or other consequential costs arising solely because an estimated production or delivery date changes.

If installation work is arranged before the complete order has arrived and been inspected, this is done at the customer's own scheduling risk.

Delivery Scheduling

Where practical, we or the delivery provider may contact you before delivery with further information about the expected delivery day or delivery window.

Delivery times during the day are normally dependent on the route, traffic conditions and the sequence of deliveries already scheduled. A time provided for delivery should therefore be treated as approximate unless expressly confirmed otherwise.

Routes can occasionally be altered at short notice because of traffic, road restrictions, vehicle issues, weather or operational requirements.

Please ensure that the telephone number and delivery details supplied with your order are accurate so that we or the delivery provider can contact you if necessary.

Access & Safe Delivery

Customers are responsible for ensuring that the delivery address is reasonably accessible for an appropriate delivery vehicle and for letting us know in advance about any restrictions that could affect delivery.

Restrictions may include narrow roads, low bridges, weight limits, controlled-access roads, parking restrictions, gated developments, steep or unsuitable access, building-site restrictions or any other circumstance that could prevent the delivery vehicle from reaching a safe unloading point.

If you are unsure whether there may be an access issue, please contact us before ordering or as soon as possible afterwards.

Rooflights can be heavy and difficult to handle. Unless a different arrangement has been expressly agreed, customers should ensure that suitable people and equipment are available where needed to receive, move and safely store the order after delivery.

The delivery driver or carrier may refuse to attempt an unsafe delivery or unloading arrangement where doing so could reasonably risk injury, property damage or damage to the goods.

Failed Or Rearranged Deliveries

If a delivery cannot be completed because nobody is available to receive it where this is required, the delivery information supplied is materially incorrect, suitable access has not been provided or a relevant access restriction was not disclosed, the order may need to be returned or redelivered.

Where additional transport, storage or redelivery costs are reasonably incurred because of those circumstances, an additional charge may apply before another delivery can be arranged.

If you become aware that an arranged delivery cannot be received, please contact us as early as possible. We cannot guarantee that a scheduled route can be changed once transport has been arranged.

This does not affect any rights you may have where a failed delivery results from our breach of contract or from the actions of a delivery provider acting on our behalf.

Checking Your Delivery

Please inspect the packaging and rooflight as soon as reasonably practical after delivery and let us know promptly if you notice visible damage, missing items or an apparent delivery discrepancy.

Where possible, photographs of the packaging, product and any visible damage can help us investigate a delivery issue more quickly.

If the packaging appears significantly damaged when the order arrives, please make the delivery driver aware where practical and record the condition of the delivery before moving or unpacking it.

Nothing in this section requires you to discover hidden damage immediately or limits any statutory rights you may have in relation to damaged, faulty, missing or incorrectly supplied goods.

Storage After Delivery

Once delivered, rooflights should be handled and stored carefully until installation.

Customers are responsible for providing a suitable and secure place for the goods after delivery and for protecting the rooflight from impact, accidental damage, unsafe handling or unsuitable storage.

Glass units should not be moved, supported or stored in a manner that places unsafe pressure on the glass, edges or sealed-unit construction.

Collection

Collection may be available by prior arrangement for suitable orders. Collection must be agreed with us before travelling to the collection location.

Please do not arrange transport or attend for collection until we have confirmed that the order is ready and that a collection time or arrangement has been agreed.

Customers collecting rooflights are responsible for providing a suitable vehicle, safe load support, restraint and handling arrangements appropriate to the size and weight of the glass unit.

Delays Outside Our Reasonable Control

Occasionally, production or delivery may be affected by circumstances outside our reasonable control.

These may include severe weather, flooding, road closures, traffic incidents, transport disruption, vehicle breakdown, supplier or glass-manufacturing disruption, shortages of materials, power or systems failures, industrial action or other events that could not reasonably have been avoided.

Where this happens, we will take reasonable steps to reduce the effect of the delay and provide updated information where practical.

Nothing in this policy removes or reduces any legal rights or remedies that apply where a delay gives you rights under applicable consumer law or under an expressly agreed contractual delivery date.

Your Statutory Rights

This Shipping & Delivery information is intended to explain how our normal production and delivery arrangements operate. It does not exclude, restrict or replace any rights you may have under applicable UK consumer law.

If a specific delivery date has been expressly agreed as binding, or if the law provides you with a particular remedy because of a delay, those rights continue to apply.
