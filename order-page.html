Technical Information

Choosing The Right Rooflight

Our frameless rooflights are factory-sealed insulated glass units designed for flat and low-pitched roof applications. The options on this page allow you to choose the glazing, safety-glass construction, finish and border that best suit your project.

Toughened or Laminated Glass?

Toughened safety glass is our standard construction and is suitable for the majority of normal flat-roof installations. It is heat-treated for increased strength and, if broken, is designed to fragment into relatively small pieces rather than large sharp shards.

Choosing Laminated upgrades the inner pane of the rooflight to laminated safety glass. A bonded interlayer helps hold the glass together if it is damaged, providing additional post-breakage protection and helping prevent broken glass from falling freely from the opening.

The additional glass, specialist interlayer and manufacturing involved are why laminated units cost more. The main benefit is the extra level of overhead-glazing safety, making laminated glass particularly useful for higher installations, steeper roof pitches and projects where additional protection beneath the rooflight is preferred.

Recommended Roof Pitch

For rooflights using our standard toughened inner glass, we recommend a maximum installation pitch of approximately 15°.

Rooflights with a laminated inner pane can be suitable for installation at pitches of up to approximately 45°, provided the supporting upstand, roof construction, perimeter support and sealing arrangement are all correctly designed and installed for the application.

For steeper installations, please refer to the Installation Guidelines section below and our full Installation Guide before fitting.

Double or Triple Glazed?

Double glazing uses two panes of glass separated by a sealed insulated cavity and provides strong thermal performance for the majority of domestic and commercial projects.

Triple glazing adds a third pane and an additional insulated cavity, improving thermal performance further and creating a more substantial insulated glass unit.

Typical centre-pane U-values are approximately 1.1 W/m²K for Double Glazing and 0.9 W/m²K for Triple Glazing.

Triple glazed units are naturally heavier than equivalent double glazed units, so the additional weight should be considered when planning handling and installation.

Insulated Glass Construction

Our rooflights use factory-sealed insulated glass construction with safety glass, warm-edge spacer technology, Low-E glass and Argon-filled cavities to help provide good thermal performance and long-term insulated-glass performance.

The precise glass thickness and construction used can vary according to the size and specification ordered. This is determined as part of the manufacturing specification, so you do not need to calculate individual glass thicknesses when choosing your rooflight.

Internal & External Sizes

All rooflight sizes shown on our website refer to the internal visible glass size.

The external top glass is larger because it also includes the ceramic border around the unit. With our standard 100mm border, the overall external dimension is normally 200mm larger than the internal dimension — 100mm on each side.

For example, a 1000 × 1500mm internal rooflight with a 100mm border has an external glass size of approximately 1200 × 1700mm.

Choosing The Border Size

Our standard 100mm ceramic border suits the majority of installations and provides coverage around a correctly constructed supporting upstand.

We also offer 125mm and 150mm borders where a wider ceramic edge is preferred or where the roof construction and upstand require additional coverage.

The ceramic border also helps conceal the supporting upstand and perimeter sealing area when the rooflight is viewed from above.

Glass Finishes

Clear provides the most neutral appearance and maximum natural-light effect.

Blue and Grey provide a tinted external appearance while continuing to allow natural daylight into the room.

Satin provides a privacy finish to the inner glass, reducing visibility through the rooflight while still allowing diffused natural light into the space below.

Self Cleaning Glass

Self Cleaning can be selected as an optional upgrade. The specialist external coating helps water spread more evenly across the glass and assists natural rainfall in breaking down and washing away everyday organic dirt.

It can reduce routine cleaning requirements, although no rooflight should be considered completely maintenance-free.

Safety & Glass Standards

Our rooflights are manufactured using established architectural glazing processes, including BS EN 12150 toughened safety glass and BS EN 1279 insulated glass unit construction where applicable.

The most appropriate specification can still depend on the building, installation height, roof pitch and intended use. If your project is unusual or you are unsure whether Toughened or Laminated is the better option, please contact us before ordering and we can help you choose.

Correct installation is just as important as choosing the right glass specification. Before fitting your rooflight, please read the Installation Guidelines section below and our full Installation Guide.

Delivery Terms & Lead Times

Availability & Delivery

Most Factory Toughened Rooflights are manufactured to order in our UK workshop when the exact size and specification required is not already available from stock.

Where your exact rooflight is already in stock and suitable delivery arrangements are available, we may be able to get it to you much sooner, with delivery possible in as little as 2 working days.

Stock, Production & Lead Times

If your rooflight is not already available from stock, it will normally be manufactured specifically for your order. Clear toughened rooflights can be available from around 4 working days, while tinted, satin and laminated specifications can take a little longer.

The estimated delivery dates shown while you configure your rooflight automatically adjust to suit the specification you select and are the best guide to the current expected timescale.

Estimated Delivery Dates

The dates shown are our best estimate based on current production requirements, working days and normal delivery scheduling. Weekends and applicable bank holidays are excluded.

If your exact rooflight is already in stock, it may be ready considerably sooner than the normal made-to-order lead time. Occasional changes can also happen during production or transport, so the dates shown should be treated as an estimated delivery window rather than a guaranteed appointment.

Delivery Charges

Delivery is calculated using your postcode together with the size and quantity of rooflights in your order. Enter your delivery postcode in the Order Summary or basket to see the applicable delivery charge before checkout.

Delivery Area

Most standard orders can be delivered across a wide area of mainland Great Britain. Very large rooflights, longer-distance deliveries or locations outside our normal delivery area may need a separate delivery quotation. If this applies, the order page will let you know.

Receiving Your Rooflight

Rooflights are substantial glass products, so please make sure somebody suitable is available to receive the delivery. Larger units can be heavy and may require more than one person or suitable lifting assistance to safely move them from the delivery vehicle.

Access To The Property

If your property has restricted access, narrow roads, low bridges, weight restrictions or anything else that may affect a delivery vehicle, please let us know before dispatch so we can help arrange the most suitable delivery option.

Checking Your Delivery

We recommend checking the rooflight and its packaging when it arrives and letting us know promptly if there is any visible transport damage or anything that does not look right.

If you believe there may be an issue with the rooflight itself, please contact us before installation wherever possible so we can help.

Collection

Collection from our Denham location can also be arranged by prior booking. Please contact us before travelling so we can confirm that your rooflight is ready and arrange a suitable collection time.
