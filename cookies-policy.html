Cookies & Website Storage

This Cookies Policy explains how Factory Toughened Rooflights uses cookies, browser storage and similar technologies when you visit or use our website, including technologies that help our shopping, ordering and website features operate correctly.

Cookies Policy
Section 01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that can be stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet or other device when you visit a website.

They can allow a website to recognise a device, remember information from an earlier page or visit, maintain a shopping basket, remember preferences or support other website functions.

Some cookies exist only while your browser is open, while others may remain on your device for a longer period.

Section 02

Similar Technologies

The rules applying to cookies can also apply to other technologies that store information on, or access information from, your device.

These may include browser storage such as local storage or session storage, scripts, tags, pixels and other technologies used to provide or support website functionality.

For simplicity, references to cookies in this policy may also include these similar storage and access technologies where appropriate.

Section 03

How We Use Cookies And Browser Storage

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate and support features of the Factory Toughened Rooflights website.

This may include remembering products or rooflight configurations added to your basket, maintaining order information while you move between pages, remembering delivery information or website preferences, supporting security and ensuring that important website functions operate correctly.

Where appropriate, we may also use technologies to understand how the website is being used so that we can identify technical problems and improve the website, its navigation and its performance.

We aim to use only the information reasonably necessary for the particular purpose for which the technology is used.

Section 04

Strictly Necessary Technologies

Some cookies or similar technologies are necessary for the website or a service requested by you to operate properly.

For example, they may be required to maintain products in a shopping basket, preserve an order configuration while moving between pages, provide security features or complete a transaction.

Where a technology is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested, we may use it without asking for consent where permitted by law.

Disabling these technologies through your browser or device settings may prevent some parts of the website from operating correctly.

Section 05

Functional And Preference Technologies

We may use technologies that allow the website to remember choices or preferences and provide functionality based on those choices.

For example, a website may remember a setting you have previously selected so that you do not have to enter or select it again unnecessarily.

Where the law allows a preference or appearance technology to be used without prior consent, we will comply with the conditions applying to that exception and provide a simple way to object where required.

Where an exception does not apply, we will obtain consent before using the technology where consent is required.

Section 06

Website Analytics And Statistics

We may use limited website usage information to understand how visitors use the Factory Toughened Rooflights website and to identify ways of improving it.

This could include information such as the number of visits to particular pages, general interactions with pages, the types of devices or browsers used and how visitors reached the website.

Where analytics or statistical technologies qualify for an exception under applicable UK law, they may be used without prior consent provided the relevant legal requirements are met.

Where such an exception requires us to provide a simple way for you to object, we will make that option available.

If an analytics or tracking technology does not qualify for an exception and requires consent, it will only be used after the required consent has been obtained.

Section 07

Advertising And Marketing Technologies

We may in the future use advertising, marketing or conversion-measurement technologies to understand the effectiveness of advertising or marketing campaigns.

Where these technologies require consent, they will not be used for those purposes unless you have made the required choice to allow them.

We will not treat continued use of the website by itself as consent to non-essential advertising or tracking technologies.

If advertising or marketing technologies are introduced or changed, this Cookies Policy and the website's cookie controls may be updated accordingly.

Section 08

Third-Party Technologies

Some website functions may be provided by third-party companies.

For example, third-party technologies may be involved in payment processing, website hosting, security, communications, analytics or other services used to operate the website.

Where a third party places or accesses information on your device through our website, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that its use is considered as part of our cookie and privacy arrangements.

Third-party providers may also process information in accordance with their own privacy or cookie information.

We may change the service providers used by the website from time to time, so this policy is not tied to a particular payment, analytics or technology provider unless it is necessary to identify one.

Section 09

Shopping Basket And Order Information

Our website may use browser storage to remember products, configurations or other information connected with your basket or order.

This allows information to remain available when you move between pages and helps prevent you from having to configure the same rooflight again each time you visit another part of the website.

This information is used to provide the shopping and ordering functionality requested by you.

Removing website data from your browser may clear your saved basket, configuration or other locally stored website information.

Section 10

Delivery And Postcode Information

Where you enter a postcode or other delivery information into the website, limited information may be stored in your browser so that delivery calculations or related website features can continue to operate as you move between pages.

This information may be used to calculate delivery availability, delivery charges or estimated delivery information.

Information required as part of an actual order may subsequently form part of the order records described in our Privacy Policy.

Section 11

How Long Cookies And Similar Information Last

Different cookies and browser-storage technologies may remain in place for different periods.

Some are temporary and are removed when the browser session ends. Others may remain for longer so that a website feature, choice or preference can be remembered on a later visit.

We aim to keep stored information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for its purpose.

Where individual cookies or technologies have specific expiry periods, these may also be shown through the website's cookie information or settings where appropriate.

Section 12

Your Cookie Choices

Where your consent is required for a particular cookie or similar technology, you will be given a choice about whether to allow it.

Where the law instead permits us to rely on an exception that requires an opportunity to object, we will provide a simple way for you to do so.

You can change or withdraw a previous choice where the relevant website controls provide that option.

Withdrawing consent will not make earlier processing carried out on the basis of valid consent unlawful.

Your Choices

You may also be able to control or remove cookies and locally stored website information through your browser or device settings. Blocking or deleting website storage may affect shopping basket, order configuration, delivery or other website functionality.

Section 13

Browser And Device Controls

Most web browsers provide settings that allow you to view, block or delete cookies and other website data.

You may also be able to remove locally stored website information through your browser or device settings.

The exact controls available depend on the browser and device you use.

Blocking or deleting all website storage may affect functionality. In particular, features such as a shopping basket, saved rooflight configuration, delivery calculations or other website functions may no longer operate as intended.

Section 14

Changes To Our Use Of Cookies

The technologies used by our website may change as the website develops.

For example, we may introduce a new payment service, analytics service, security feature or other website functionality.

We will review our use of cookies and similar technologies periodically and update this policy where appropriate.

Where a change means that new consent is required, we will obtain that consent before using the relevant technology for the new purpose.

Section 15

Privacy And Personal Information

Some information collected through cookies or similar technologies may constitute personal information.

Where personal information is involved, our use of that information is also subject to applicable data protection law.
