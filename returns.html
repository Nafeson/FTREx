Returns & Product Issues

We want every Factory Toughened Rooflights order to arrive in the condition and specification you expect. If something does not look right when your order arrives, please let us know as soon as possible and we will work with you to understand the issue and arrange the appropriate next step.

Returns & Product Issues

Our Approach

Rooflights are large, specialist glass products, so we try to make our returns process as straightforward as possible while making sure any issue is properly understood before a product is moved or returned.

If your rooflight has arrived damaged, appears defective or does not match the size or specification you ordered, please contact us and we will help you through the next steps.

Our normal returns process asks customers to contact us within 14 days of delivery where there is an issue with the product.

Nothing on this page removes or reduces any statutory rights that may apply to your purchase.

Please Check Your Order On Arrival

Where reasonably possible, please take a few moments to inspect the rooflight and its packaging when it arrives.

We recommend checking:

  • That the glass has not been visibly damaged in transit.
  • That the number of rooflights delivered matches your order.
  • That the rooflight appears to be the size and specification ordered.
  • That the packaging has not suffered significant visible damage.

If you notice an obvious problem while the driver is still present, please make them aware of it where practical and contact us as soon as possible.

Visible Damage On Delivery

If damage is clearly visible when the rooflight arrives, noting the issue at the time of delivery helps us investigate what happened and resolve the matter more quickly. Accepting a delivery without noting visible damage does not automatically remove your legal rights, but we may need additional information or evidence to establish when the damage occurred.

When To Contact Us

Please contact us as soon as possible, and normally within 14 days of delivery, if:

  • Your rooflight has arrived damaged.
  • The product appears to have a manufacturing defect.
  • The rooflight delivered is a different size from the size ordered.
  • The glazing, finish or other product specification does not match your order.
  • An item is missing from the delivery.

If the issue is visible before installation, please leave the rooflight uninstalled and unaltered while we investigate it.

What We May Ask You To Send Us

Most delivery or product issues can be assessed much more quickly from a few clear photographs.

When contacting us, please include where possible:

  • Your name and order details.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Photographs showing the complete rooflight.
  • Close-up photographs of any damage or defect.
  • Photographs of the packaging if there is evidence of transit damage.
  • A photograph showing a tape measure where the issue relates to size.

We may ask for additional photographs or information if this is reasonably necessary to understand the issue.

Please Do Not Install A Product With A Visible Issue

If you can see that the rooflight is damaged, the wrong size, the wrong specification or otherwise appears incorrect before fitting, please contact us before installation.

Installing, bonding, altering or incorporating the rooflight into the building can make it considerably more difficult to inspect the original condition of the product.

Before Installation

Where an issue is visible beforehand, please keep the rooflight uninstalled until we have had an opportunity to review the problem with you.

This does not remove any rights you may have where a fault could not reasonably have been identified before installation.

If Your Rooflight Arrives Damaged

Please contact us promptly and send photographs showing the damage and, where relevant, the surrounding packaging.

We will review the information and determine the most appropriate way to resolve the issue.

Depending on the circumstances, this may involve replacement, collection, repair, refund or another suitable resolution.

Please do not dispose of the rooflight or packaging until we have confirmed whether we need any further information from you.

If We Have Supplied The Wrong Product

If the rooflight supplied does not match the dimensions or specification confirmed in your order, please let us know as soon as possible.

We will compare the delivered product against your order record and may ask you for photographs or measurements to help confirm the difference.

Where we have supplied an incorrect product, we will work with you to provide an appropriate remedy.

If You Believe The Product Is Defective

If you believe there is a manufacturing or product fault, please send us photographs together with a description of what you have noticed.

Some characteristics of glass, ceramic borders, reflections and manufacturing tolerances can be normal, so we may need to review the issue before confirming whether the product is defective.

Where a genuine defect is confirmed, we will arrange the appropriate remedy in accordance with the circumstances and your applicable rights.

If The Wrong Size Was Ordered

All sizes shown on our website refer to the internal visible glass size unless expressly stated otherwise.

If a rooflight has been manufactured correctly to the dimensions selected or supplied with the order, we are normally unable to accept it as a return simply because the structural opening was measured incorrectly or the wrong size was selected.

If you are unsure whether the supplied size matches the order, please contact us before installation and we can help you check it.

Our Fitting & Installation page contains further information about internal glass sizes, structural openings and external rooflight dimensions.

Custom & Made-To-Specification Rooflights

Many rooflights are manufactured specifically to the customer's chosen dimensions or specification.

Where a rooflight has been genuinely manufactured to custom measurements or a personalised specification, change-of-mind cancellation and return rights may not apply in the same way as they do to standard products.

This does not affect your rights where the product is faulty, damaged, incorrectly supplied or otherwise does not conform to the order.

Standard Products

Where statutory cancellation rights apply to a standard product purchased online, those rights continue to apply in accordance with applicable law.

If you wish to discuss returning a standard product, please contact us before arranging transport.

Because rooflights are large and fragile glass products, they should not be sent back using an unapproved courier or transport method without first agreeing the return arrangements with us.

Returning A Rooflight

Please do not send a rooflight back to our business address without first contacting us.

Once we have reviewed the issue, we will let you know whether the product needs to be returned and how the return should be handled.

Where the return is required because we supplied a damaged, defective or incorrect product, the appropriate return or collection arrangements will be discussed with you.

Where a return is not the result of a fault or error on our part, the customer may be responsible for appropriate return transport where permitted by law.

Refunds

Where a refund is agreed, it will normally be made back to the original payment method.

The timing of the payment appearing in your account may depend on the bank or payment provider used for the original transaction.

Where we reasonably need to inspect or receive the returned product before completing a refund, we will let you know.

Nothing in this section changes any refund timescale required by applicable law.

Replacement Rooflights

Where a replacement is the appropriate solution, we will confirm the replacement specification with you before it is manufactured or dispatched.

Replacement lead times can depend on the size, glazing specification and current manufacturing schedule.

We will aim to keep you informed of the expected replacement timeframe while the order is being resolved.

Damage After Delivery

Once the rooflight has been delivered, please handle and store it carefully until installation.

We cannot normally accept responsibility for damage caused after delivery by impact, incorrect storage, unsafe handling, unsuitable support, installation damage, alteration or the use of incompatible materials.

If you are unsure whether damage was present when the product arrived, please contact us and provide as much information as possible.

How We Resolve Product Issues

Every situation can be slightly different, particularly with large made-to-order glass products.

Once we have reviewed the order details and the information you provide, we will explain the next step.

Depending on the circumstances, the appropriate outcome may be:

  • A replacement product.
  • A repair or other corrective solution where appropriate.
  • Collection or return of the affected rooflight.
  • A full or partial refund where appropriate.
  • Another solution agreed with you.

Our aim is to resolve genuine problems fairly and as efficiently as reasonably possible.
